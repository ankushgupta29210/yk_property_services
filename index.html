    Common questions

    Good to know before we begin

    Quick answers to what people ask us most. Anything else, just call or message.

    Do you work on both homes and commercial properties?

    Yes. Services are structured for homeowners, landlords, property managers and Yellowknife businesses.

    Can you handle several jobs in one visit?

    Yes. Send us your complete list so we can organize painting, repairs, cleaning and maintenance together.

    Do you provide estimates first?

    Yes. Every property is different, so we review the work and provide a clear written estimate before it begins. Estimates are free.

    Can I send photos with my request?

    Yes. The online quote form accepts a project photo, and you can send as many as you like on WhatsApp or by email.

    Do you offer recurring maintenance?

    Yes. Recurring plans can be arranged for homeowners, landlords, property managers and commercial properties.

    Can you paint in winter?

    Yes. Interior painting, drywall and repairs run all year — winter is a great time for indoor work. Exterior painting and staining are booked for the warmer months.

    What are your hours?

    Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM. Closed Sundays, but you can send a quote request or email any time.
